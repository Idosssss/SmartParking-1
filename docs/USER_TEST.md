# User test

A short test with people who did **not** build the project, to find out what is unclear.
Fill in the results table after you have run it; do not leave it empty in a submission.

## How to run it (10 minutes per person)

1. Pick 3 people who have never seen the project (a classmate from another team, a family member...).
2. Give them **only** the one-page guide ([USER_GUIDE.md](USER_GUIDE.md)) and the connected board. Do not explain anything.
3. Ask them to do these tasks while you watch and say nothing; write down where they hesitate or get stuck:
   1. Connect the board to the site.
   2. Make a "car" arrive and follow the directions to the spot.
   3. Tell you whether the spot is free or taken just by looking at the board.
   4. Something is wrong: pull the USB cable. What do they think happened, and what would they do?
   5. Find out where to change how close a car must be to be detected.
4. Ask: What was confusing? What would you change? Anything you expected that was missing?
5. Time each task.
